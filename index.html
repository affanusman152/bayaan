The Society

Bayaan is the umbrella under which every form of expression at FAST NUCES Multan lives — the argued word, the performed word, the written word.

Bayaan began as a simple idea: a campus needs a place where people are heard. Not just the loudest — the sharpest, the softest, the funniest, the ones still finding the courage to stand up. Placeholder copy — replace with the society's real founding note.

Today we run debates and declamations, send delegations to MUNs, stage plays, publish student writing, and turn ordinary Tuesday evenings into open mics.
